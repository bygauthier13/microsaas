## 4. Why customers would switch (or add us)

1. **It's the law now, and their current tools don't do it.** The pain is new; there's no incumbent habit to break.
2. **The landlord loop.** Agents' biggest delay is landlord approval; an approval link that shows the statutory deadline (and records the decision) solves a problem no CRM addresses.
3. **Letters in minutes, correctly.** The written summary and delay notice have required content; the generator + checklist removes legal anxiety.
4. **Proof on demand.** The append-only timeline and one-click evidence pack replace reconstructing email threads for a tribunal or an angry landlord.
5. **Low commitment.** No card for the trial, CSV import from their CRM, runs alongside existing systems, monthly billing, data exportable.
