# Emails to send

The four outreach emails, ready to copy into Gmail. The strategy, list building and replies are
explained in [FIRST_10_CUSTOMERS.md](../FIRST_10_CUSTOMERS.md).

## How to send them

- **Fill in the fields** in `{curly brackets}`:
  - `{First name}`: the contact's first name. Without one, write "Hi there".
  - `{Agency}`: the agency's trading name.
  - `{Deadline}`: 10 working days after the day you send, from the table below.
  - `{Your name}`: your first and last name.
- **Timing:** email 1 on day 0, email 2 on day 4, email 3 on day 9, email 4 on day 16. **Stop as
  soon as someone replies**, and answer them by hand.
- **How many a day:** 10 a day in week 1, 20 a day in week 2, then 30 to 40. Never more than 50
  a day from this address.
- **Only companies:** Ltd, LLP or Scottish partnerships. Skip sole traders (UK law needs their
  consent first).
- **Plain text only:** no images, no attachments, one link per email. Keep the "no thanks" line,
  and honour it the same day.
- **Optional:** set your Gmail sender name to "{Your name} · RepairClock" (Settings → Accounts →
  Send mail as → edit info), so the brand shows above the subject.
